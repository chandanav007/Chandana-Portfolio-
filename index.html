About Me

Where financial operations meet data.

I work where accuracy matters most — inside the transactions that keep banking running.

As a Transaction Officer at Mphasis since 21 May 2026, I support banking and financial transaction processing in line with established procedures. My day-to-day work draws on knowledge of debit card and credit card e-disputes and chargeback workflows — reviewing disputed transaction details and supporting documentation, and assisting with transaction investigation and exception handling.

I am familiar with the payment rails behind modern banking, including ACH, SWIFT, Fedwire and CHIPS, and I follow operational procedures and quality requirements with close attention to detail.

Alongside operations, I have trained in data analytics with SQL, Python, Microsoft Excel, Power BI and Tableau. I am genuinely interested in using data to solve business problems — turning raw numbers into clear, decision-ready insight — and I have applied this through hands-on projects in data cleaning, analysis and dashboard development.
